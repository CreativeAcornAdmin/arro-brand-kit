Palette System — The Buyer Journey

The ARRO graphic language is fixed — logo, typography, layout, and structure never change. What changes is color. Each stage of the buyer's journey has its own palette, acting as a progress signal: the visual environment itself tells the buyer how far they've come.

"Oh, I made it to the yellow stage." — color as reward, not decoration.

Stage 1
Explore
Buyer arrives. Orienting. Building trust. Learning what ARRO is and whether it's for them.
Palette
#547571 · #79ADA8
Calm. Trustworthy. No pressure.
Stage 2
Learn
Building readiness. Understanding the process. Score is climbing. Confidence is forming.
Palette
#9B7520 · #C9A030
Warming. Aspirational. Premium feel.
Stage 3
Ready
Score is strong. Matched with brokers. Actively exploring homes. In motion.
Palette
#BF5A18 · #E88A4A
Energized. Momentum. Let's go.
Stage 4
Home
Offer accepted. Closing. Or closed. The buyer has arrived. Time to celebrate.
Palette
#6B3E7A · #9B72AE
Rich. Celebratory. Memorable.
The Temperature Arc
Cool · Calm
Warm · Building
Hot · In Motion
Rich · Arrived
Design principle: The graphic language never changes — the logo, the type, the layout, the grid. Color alone shifts. This means a buyer in Stage 3 instantly knows they're somewhere different from Stage 1, without anything being explained to them. The environment does the communicating.
